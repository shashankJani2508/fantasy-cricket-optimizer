Part	B:	Specification
1.	What	the	scoring	engine	is
The	scoring	engine	is	a	
calculator
.	For	one	match	and	a	given	list	of	players,	it	predicts	each	player’s	
expected	Dream11	points
	(the
“base	score”)	by	applying	the	
rubric
:	8	factors	combined	by	a	weighted	average.
It	is	
code	only
.	No	AI,	no	randomness.	The	same	inputs	always	give	the	same	outputs.
It	does	
not
	choose	players,	captains	or	teams.	It	does	
not
	read	news.	Those	are	later	steps	(Gemini	moderator,	optimizer).
Its	output	is	saved	to	Neon	and	later	passed	to	Gemini	and	the	optimizer.
Analogy:	the	rubric	is	the	marking	scheme;	the	scoring	engine	is	the	examiner	who	applies	it	mechanically	to	every	player.
